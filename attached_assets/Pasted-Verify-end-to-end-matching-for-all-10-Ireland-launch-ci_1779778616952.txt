Verify end-to-end matching for all 10 Ireland launch cities.

Context:
This is NOT about public city pages or 24h delayed previews.
This is about the actual app/realtime flow:
ingestion → city normalization → stored listing city → user search profile filters → matching → user_matches → notifications eligibility.

Launch cities:
- Dublin
- Cork
- Galway
- Limerick
- Waterford
- Kilkenny
- Swords
- Bray
- Drogheda
- Dundalk

Prove that if a customer creates an alert/search profile for any of these cities, matching works correctly and only relevant listings for that city are matched.
