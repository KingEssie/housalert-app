Unify onboarding/signup branding across HousAlert.

The flow looks different from the branded flow used elsewhere.

IMPORTANT:
Do not change authentication logic.
Do not change signup logic.
Do not change search profile creation logic.
Only unify UI/branding and route consistency.

Goal:
Every onboarding/signup route must use the same HousAlert app branding and components.

- Purple: #bbadfb

- Green: #85fb8c
- Green icon/text: #223546
- Dark navy: #223546
- Black: #111111
- App background: #f6f6f6
