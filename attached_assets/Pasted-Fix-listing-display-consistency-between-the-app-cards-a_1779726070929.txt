Fix listing display consistency between the app cards and the public Duda widget.

Context:
The public Duda widget and the app are showing inconsistent listing data.

Examples:
- The website/widget can show a better street title like “Mary Street” or “The Rise”.
- The app sometimes still shows generic location titles like “Dublin 24, Dublin” or “Blackrock, Dublin”.
- The app sometimes shows bedrooms, but the widget does not.
- The widget should show 3 pills where available: bedrooms, size m², price.
- Emails should NOT be changed in this task.

Goal:
Create consistent listing display normalization for:
1. app listing cards
2. public latest-listings API
3. Duda widget

Do NOT touch:
- push notifications
- service worker
- Stripe
- matching pipeline
- ingestion scheduler
- email templates
- Germany infrastructure

  “Dublin 24, Dublin”
  “Blackrock, Dublin”

- If no better value exists, fallback safely.

- “Apartment 68 The Plaza Shangan Road, Dublin” → “The Plaza Shangan Road”
